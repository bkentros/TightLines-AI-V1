# Michigan River Run Spot Finder access audit

Audited: 2026-09-08

## Decision

Expand the seven supported Michigan River Run inventories from 81 to 95
source-listed public access points. The release adds 14 entries, corrects one
conflated Pere Marquette record, separates the two distinct Bass River launches,
and corrects Grand River Park's access types. No River Run reach, seasonal-zone
rule, or biological recommendation changes as part of this work.

The inventory remains intentionally narrower than every place where an angler
might physically reach water. A location is included only when a government,
land manager, or government-supported water-trail source identifies public
access and the site falls inside the existing species corridor. Private
liveries, informal pull-offs, road shoulders, canoe-in camps, sites beyond
barriers, planned-but-unbuilt facilities, and ambiguous bank access remain
excluded.

## Important exclusions

- **Pere Marquette — Lower Branch Bridge:** the Forest Service plan documents
  walk-in/carry-in use but also explicitly identifies boat-access safety
  concerns and an incomplete improvement action. It is not promoted as a viable
  subscriber access point in this release.
- **Pere Marquette — Elk and Logmark:** these are canoe-oriented rest/camp sites
  without ordinary public vehicle access. Jorgenson's Tract was also omitted
  because its access identity was unresolved in the cited management plan.
- **Grand — Bur Oak Landing:** public ownership and fishing are documented, but
  the open-space page does not establish a designated river launch or a reliably
  defined fishing entrance. It remains excluded rather than implying an access
  path through floodplain/wetland frontage.
- **Grand Rapids — Fish Ladder conceptual improvements:** future angling and
  boating elements shown in planning material are not treated as current
  facilities.
- **White Lake municipal launches:** Montague and other lake launches are valid
  public facilities, but they are not river access points and would dilute the
  lower White River section.
- **Sites above Homestead, Tippy, Croton, Hesperia, or outside Michigan on the
  St. Joseph:** excluded because they fall outside the engine's supported
  migratory corridor or state scope.

## Product rules preserved

- Entries describe access, not the “best spot” or live fish location.
- A public site does not guarantee legal parking, open roads, safe wading,
  navigability, or permission to cross adjacent land.
- Boat, carry-in, shore, walk-in, platform and wade labels are included only
  when the source supports that exact amenity.
- Fee, permit, dam, exposed-pier, seasonal, hunting and rough-access cautions
  remain visible where relevant.
- Lists remain ordered downstream to upstream inside the existing River Run
  sections.
