# Washington Fishability calibration audit — 2026-08-31

Fishability is a species-independent presentation-shape read from the accepted primary flow gauge. It does not measure migration, abundance, access, safety, turbidity, or catch probability. Bands are fixed to the named gauge reach and are not transferred elsewhere on the river.

## Puyallup / Clarks lower-mainstem reach

- Source: USGS 12101500 approved daily discharge.
- Calibration window: July 15-December 5 for each calendar year, 1988-2025.
- Coverage: 5,472 of 5,472 expected dates (100%); 38 years.
- Distribution (CFS): minimum 430; p5 935.1; p25 1,380; median 1,850; p75 2,620; p95 5,334.5; p99 11,858; maximum 36,400.
- Rounded bands: very low below 940; low 940-1,380; ideal 1,380-2,620; high-fishable 2,620-5,330; very high 5,330-11,900; blown out at 11,900 and above.
- Scope: lower mainstem at river mile 6.6 near Puyallup/Clarks Creek. The result does not infer glacial turbidity and is not extrapolated to the Carbon confluence.

## Cowlitz / Castle Rock lower-mainstem reach

- Source: USGS 14243000 approved daily discharge.
- Calibration window: July 15-February 15 seasonal union for 2001-2025.
- Coverage: 5,400 of 5,400 expected dates (100%); 25 years.
- Distribution (CFS): minimum 2,350; p5 2,840; p25 3,830; median 5,860; p75 12,200; p95 21,500; p99 34,100; maximum 87,200.
- Rounded bands: very low below 2,840; low 2,840-3,830; ideal 3,830-12,200; high-fishable 12,200-21,500; very high 21,500-34,100; blown out at 34,100 and above.
- Record decision: USGS documents October 1985-April 2000 as a seasonal-record interval. The calibration excludes that incomplete interval and uses the complete modern 2001-2025 record.
- Scope: lower mainstem at Castle Rock, downstream of the Toutle River. It is not the Barrier Dam tailwater or a project-release safety read.

## Fixed score safeguards

Both calibrations retain the engine's conservative caps: stale gauge 55, unknown trend 49, very low 45, sharp rise in high water 40, and blown out 24. A missing/older-than-24-hours gauge remains unavailable.
