# PierCast remaining-species decision register

This register evaluates nine species against the seven covered structures in five cities. It records eligibility decisions and unresolved calibration explicitly. None of the 45 pairings has a justified numeric onboarding configuration in this review. Excluded means excluded from this product roster on the reviewed evidence, not biologically absent. Narrow-season status preserves a credible local fishery lead; it does not certify a major current target or numerical readiness.

The strongest additional leads are yellow perch at Manistee North Pier, summer perch at Ludington North Breakwater, and channel-facing freshwater drum at Grand Haven. Exact seasonal magnitudes, comparative 1–10 calibration and thermal ordinates remain unresolved. The completed 20 core curves and public-release gates remain unchanged.

## Evidence and numerical method

The preserved Michigan query contains 12,996 estimates for four ports and Pier/Dock mode, including all species and effort rows. Catch includes retained and released fish from 1997 onward. The long (1997–2022), modern (2012–2022) and recent (2018–2022) comparisons all exclude 2020. A period label is a requested range, not a promise that every year was sampled. The public catalog still points to this same dashboard. [^1][^56]

Rates below are the sum of published catch estimates divided by the sum of positive all-species Pier/Dock angler-hours in matched year/month strata, multiplied by 1,000. They are neither individual-trip CPUE nor target-specific catch per hour. Recurrence counts years with positive estimated catch among years with explicit catch and positive effort. Missing species rows remain missing; explicit zeros remain zeros. Missing winter surveys never become absent fish or a 1.0 score. Lake-trout summary strata require both named Lean and Fat components, making that combined summary incomplete where a component is omitted; inspect the raw species rows before drawing a negative conclusion.

The tables show the two strongest modern months as diagnostic leads, including zero months when no positive modern signal exists. This selection is not a seasonal-window estimator. The complete 1,872-row monthly artifact includes all months and all 13 species for comparison. Unequal matched years, total-mode effort, released fish, unknown sizes and changing target shares prevent a direct cross-species score conversion. Largest-year catch shares are retained in that artifact to expose peaks dominated by one year.

The 2025 Michigan supplement separates Pier/Dock but pools Lake Michigan sites. Its report explains that interview location, target species and fishing method are collected; their existence in agency records does not make them available in this aggregate. Historical interval-count corrections concern boat counts (Table A3); no unsupported adjustment is applied to this Pier/Dock extract. [^2][^3]

Wisconsin Tables 6 distinguish pier from shore, stream and boat, but pool Lake Michigan and Green Bay. Sheboygan County effort cannot be joined to those regional mode totals as though a city-by-mode cross-tab existed. Harvest is not total catch for released bass/drum. The 2024 reduced survey and modeled spring estimates cannot establish Sheboygan spring or winter opportunity. [^4][^5][^6]

Current stocking confirms lake trout in the Michigan harbor/reef context, including Ludington marina releases, and offshore Sheboygan Reef stocking. These are important population context, but no stocking count is converted into adult pier opportunity. [^55][^54]

The frozen core scale reaches 10.0 at Manistee steelhead and uses explicitly provisional calibration. Thousands of perch per 1,000 mixed-target hours do not prove an opportunity many times better than steelhead; fish size, schooling, methods and target effort differ. A nonbinding salmonid log-CPUE guide cannot establish a universal transformation for the nine additions. Assessment research also cautions that catchability varies by method, place and time. No new ordering against the core peaks is asserted without an applicable calibration basis. [^58]

## Contradictions and conditions for reopening

Grand Haven lake-whitefish historical harvest is not a lawful-method bite series. The agency documents snagged-fish contributions and a November single unweighted hook restriction; the current regulation covers the port from the westernmost pierhead to US-31. A November 2024 zero-catch jigging report also prevents cherry-picking only successful historical weeks. A current method-specific South Pier series is required. [^8][^9][^46]

Frankfort menominee reporting published in 2026 recalls a trip about ten years earlier. That date must not become a 2026 occurrence. Unspecified whitefish, bass and harbor fishing remain taxonomically or geographically unresolved. [^11][^21][^28]

The bulletin ledger preserves retrieved dates and source hashes. The July 2023 bulletin 364ea3a has an inconsistent title: the send/body date is July 12. Repeated adjacent weekly reports are not independent annual samples. Reports describing low catches, size sorting and slow fishing remain part of the evidence; warm-season bycatch is not automatically intentional targeting.

Search leads with inaccessible full text—including the AFS 2023 local-activities page—and generic commercial fishing pages do not supply configuration evidence. A failed retrieval or no matching report is an evidence limitation, not proof that a fishery never exists. Regional stocking, federal/agency biology and NOAA surface forecasts cannot fill an exact-pier target-effort gap.

Priority evidence needed: exact station/structure coding, north/south and fishing-face identification, target species, completed-trip hours, method, retained/released counts and sizes, successful and unsuccessful trips, sampling coverage and uncertainty, and contemporary lawful-method context. Agency-held records or prospective observation could resolve these gaps. No agency messages have been sent. No artificial seasonal floor, copied city curve or fabricated thermal ordinate is an acceptable substitute.
