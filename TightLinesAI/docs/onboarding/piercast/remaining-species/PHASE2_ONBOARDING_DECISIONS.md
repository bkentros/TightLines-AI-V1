# PierCast Phase 2 onboarding decisions

Phase 2 admits seven additional city/species pairings into the private provisional scored lineup and records final deferrals for the other 38 of the 45 researched pairings. Nine of those deferrals retain Phase 1 annual research curves; the other 29 did not pass Phase 1 annual calibration. A deferral is a decision based on the current evidence, not a claim of biological absence.

The admission standard is repeated pier-catch evidence plus named covered-structure corroboration or a bounded, explicit side inference supported by the local source chain. Thermal curves remain transparent product calibrations of compatibility, not empirical bite probabilities. This uses the completed core species’ private-review standard. It does not approve a lakeward surface cell as fish-experienced or bottom temperature, and it does not enable public ratings.

## Compatibility and remaining validation

The versioned private roster contains 27 city/species pairs, producing 135 shadow forecasts across five dates. New daily snapshots use the city-specific rosters. Legacy four-species snapshots remain readable and keep their first-write daily lock; deploying the new lineup does not replace a previously committed day. New seasonal/thermal provenance identifiers identify the assembled private lineup while the original core curves retain their IDs.

The nine deferred annual candidates remain in a separate private research collection, with round whitefish retaining no combined thermal hypothesis. They do not enter the scored lineup or headline. Public catalogs remain empty, profile ratingEnabled stays false, temperature representation remains blocked, and public/outcome-validation requirements remain unchanged.

The current regulation review applies through March 31, 2027. Bass opportunity includes catch-and-immediate-release outside the possession season. Grand Haven November hook restrictions apply across all species, including the admitted drum and largemouth. Access closures remain separate; the roster is not a guarantee of lawful physical access or a claim that any method is allowed. Expired regulation review sets private targeting eligibility to unknown.

Phase 3 compares the annual lineup together: relative magnitudes, overlapping peaks, low-opportunity periods and city differences. It must not fill seasonal gaps by forcing unsupported additions. Public release requires separate explicit authorization and the existing scientific gates.
