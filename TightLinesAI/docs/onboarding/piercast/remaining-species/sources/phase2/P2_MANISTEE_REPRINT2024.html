Fishing report shows local conditions, best methods

By Arielle Breen, Assistant EditorJune 28, 2024
Manistee
Ludington
Frankfort

The Department of Natural Resources’ weekly fishing report for June 26 shows some of the most current conditions in Manistee, Mason and Benzie counties. 

Manistee

In Manistee, the report for Lake Michigan says anglers are mainly seeing a mix of Chinook salmon, coho salmon, steelhead and lake trout along the shelf at various depths.

“Depths included 80-140 feet of water and out to 180-220 feet of water when fishing 45-120 feet down,” the report notes. “Spoons, flasher-fly combinations and meat rigs all worked well.”

On the Manistee North Pier, bowfin and freshwater drum were caught inside the harbor when anglers used lures, alewife and crawlers. 

“On the lake side, a couple steelhead were caught while using spawn. Early morning and late-night bites seemed to be best,” the report concludes.

Ludington

Anglers are catching a mix of small salmon, a lake trout or two, and a couple steelhead were caught straight out, south and northwest of Ludington up to Big Sable Point in 70-100 feet of water. 

Advertisement

Article continues below this ad

Anglers also found luck when going out to160 feet of water when fishing 60 to 80 feet down. 

“Fishing was hit or miss; only a few large Chinook salmon were in the mix. The piers were reported to have been slow,” the report concludes.

Frankfort

In Frankfort, reports indicate anglers were starting to pick up a few mature Chinook salmon out front and trolling north to the point.

“Both blue and green spoons and green flies were reported to have worked best,” the report reads in part. “Very good numbers and sizes of lake trout were reported in Platte Bay and Six Mile Hole by anglers trolling and jigging.”

Some reports showed small Chinook salmon in the area that are not tripping releases, so the DNR is advising anglers to check their lines often.
