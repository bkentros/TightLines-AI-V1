# Sheboygan PierCast Dossier

**Covered:** North Pier and South Pier\
**Published access:** yes\
**Live access:** not checked; user must follow current signs and conditions

## Routes and identity

North Pier: use Deland Park, 715 Broughton Dr, Sheboygan, WI 53081
(`43.7521094427185, -87.7037831990455`). The City lists fishing and the
Wisconsin DNR identifies the North Pier base in Deland Park.

South Pier: use South Pier Turnaround, 226 South Pier Dr, Sheboygan, WI 53081
(`43.7482052405373, -87.7040245790135`). The City explicitly says this public
waterfront provides access for pier walking and pier fishing; Wisconsin DNR
independently identifies the South Pier access.

## Frozen species

Chinook salmon, Coho salmon, Steelhead, and Brown trout are candidate products
with frozen provisional curves. Coho is no longer marked unresolved: the
completed v0.3/v0.4 evidence record combines current local relevance, repeated
structure-specific local records, and regional pier-mode estimates. Contemporary
Sheboygan-only monthly effort remains a confidence limitation and
outcome-validation requirement, not a missing-species condition.

## Temperature validation

LMHOFS cell `(215,37)` remains provisional. GLOS `obs_709` produced no QC-good
records in the audited series. Independent sources
`sheboygan_north_pier__surface_logger_v1` and
`sheboygan_south_pier__surface_logger_v1` are required to verify both sides of
