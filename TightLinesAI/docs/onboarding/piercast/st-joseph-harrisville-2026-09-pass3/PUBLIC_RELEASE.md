# PierCast public release

On September 20, 2026, the complete 27-city PierCast catalog was approved for public research access.

The final promotion added these five Michigan cities:

- St. Joseph
- South Haven
- Holland
- Lexington
- Harrisville

They are included in public catalogs, standings, and authenticated city reports under release policy `piercast-public-research-v3-2026-09-20-twenty-seven-city`. Existing access rules remain unchanged: free accounts receive four lifetime city/day reports, paid accounts can open every released report, and owner-review routes remain private.

The installed iOS and Android clients fetch the PierCast catalog dynamically, so the backend promotion exposes the 27-city roster without requiring a binary update. FinFindr 1.14 is also being released to both stores so the corrected full-width “Piers covered” rows and compact bottom-of-report access notice reach installed users.
