# Grand Haven PierCast Dossier

**Covered:** South Pier only\
**Published access:** yes\
**Live access:** not checked; user must follow park hours, signs, and conditions

## Route and identity

Use Grand Haven State Park, 1001 S Harbor Dr, Grand Haven, MI 49417; access
reference `43.05618, -86.24778`. Michigan DNR identifies the adjacent pier and
boardwalk at the Grand River mouth as popular with anglers.

North Pier / Fisherman's Pier is excluded because of the active construction
closure. An expected completion year is not a reopening and cannot activate it
automatically.

## Frozen species

Chinook salmon, Coho salmon, Steelhead, and Brown trout are candidate products
with frozen provisional curves.

## Temperature validation

LMHOFS cell `(146,180)` remains provisional. GLOS `obs_671` is seasonal and
offshore of the pier; local source `grand_haven_south_pier__surface_logger_v1`
must test lake-side and Grand River plume transfer.
