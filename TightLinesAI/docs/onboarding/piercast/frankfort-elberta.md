# Frankfort–Elberta PierCast Dossier

**Covered:** Frankfort North Breakwater and Elberta South Breakwater\
**Published access:** yes\
**Live access:** not checked; user must follow current signs and conditions

## Routes and identity

Frankfort: use the Lake Michigan public beach at the end of Main Street / Father
Marquette Circle, Frankfort, MI 49635 (`44.63204547, -86.24476064`). City
records identify the public beachfront and existing pier sidewalk; the M-22
byway lists fishing and dawn-to-dusk use.

Elberta: use the public Elberta Beach route from the parking area at the base of
Bye Road, Elberta, MI 49628 (`44.62625025, -86.24244564`). The Village says the
beach starts at the Elberta Pier, publishes the route, and warns that pier
access is at the visitor's own risk.

## Frozen species

Chinook salmon, Coho salmon, Steelhead, and Brown trout are candidate products
with frozen provisional curves. Frankfort Chinook is no longer conditional:
official Pier/Dock estimates and direct breakwall reports establish shore
reachability. Outcome validation still controls the magnitude.

## Temperature validation

LMHOFS cell `(303,180)` remains provisional. No qualifying pier-local public
temperature feed was found. Independent sources
`frankfort_north_breakwater__surface_logger_v1` and
`elberta_south_breakwater__surface_logger_v1` are required to test both
structure transfer and Betsie plume effects.
