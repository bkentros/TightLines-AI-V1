## Evidence contract

PierCast's established product boundary is **one general reading for the city harbor and its principal public piers**, with structure and subarea notes where needed. A fish taken from a river well upstream, a charter or launched boat, an offshore reef, or another town in the same county does not by itself establish a harbor-pier fishery. A fish observed by electrofishing establishes local presence, not angler catch rate. Stocking establishes management input, not a pier catch. A weekly field note establishes a dated observation, not an annual mean. A grouped “salmon” or “rainbow/brown trout” label is a research lead for each plausible species, but does not measure each one. A historic single catch can help identify a research lead; it cannot set a score.

The machine-readable [decision matrix](species-decisions.json) reviews **all 19 current PierCast species at all five cities (95 decisions)**. `advance` means a defensible Pass 2 research candidate; it is **not** a score, a final Formula v3 Grade A/B admission, or a promise of public display. `hold` names a plausible occurrence lead needing local fishing-mode evidence. `exclude` means no credible local pier lead was found in the reviewed collection, **not** biological absence. A later source can reopen any decision. No pair has numeric approval yet. [Source ledger](source-ledger.json) records publisher, geography, method, dates, permitted uses, and limits.

**Why the Wisconsin research roster is wider.** The DNR access guide names rainbow/brown trout at Two Rivers harbor; brown trout and pike at Kewaunee river mouth; rainbow trout and pike at Algoma river mouth; and smallmouth, pike and rainbow/brown trout at Manitowoc Marina. Its three species columns are not a full inventory. The 2026 weekly DNR report says Algoma and Kewaunee pier anglers targeted *trout and salmon*, names Chinook in Kewaunee's harbor/pier paragraph, and records pier-caught pike, smallmouth and roughly ten Chinook in **Manitowoc County**. A 2010 outdoor report compiled from WDNR and private observations names kings at Two Rivers, Manitowoc and Kewaunee piers, smallmouth at Kewaunee pier, perch at Manitowoc harbor/south pier, and a rainbow at Algoma south pier. A 2019 local report independently names a Two Rivers pier Chinook. These older reports are exact-place occurrence evidence, not current strength estimates. Boat catches remain excluded. The 2026 Algoma perch and smallmouth observations are attributed to **Ahnapee River anglers**, so those species stay leads pending breakwall/harbor-edge proof. [DNR access guide](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/ClosetoHomeFishing.pdf); [DNR field report](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Fishing/lakemichigan/OutdoorReport); [2010 compiled outdoor report](https://wisconsinoutdoor.com/smf/index.php?topic=4046.0); [2019 local report](https://www.seehafernews.com/2019/09/07/outdoor-report-2/).

**What the Wisconsin creel tables can and cannot say.** DNR's county-by-mode salmonid tables show repeat pier harvest in both Kewaunee and Manitowoc counties. In 2022/2023/2024 the county pier Chinook estimates were 28/219/53 (Kewaunee) and 136/497/128 (Manitowoc); brown trout were 29/13/36 and 20/0/50; rainbow trout were 0/0/133 and 0/0/116; coho were 0/0/24 and 20/205/133. Both counties' **lake-trout pier estimates were zero in 2022–24**, but historical pier estimates include Kewaunee 1/59/10 in 2012/2015/2016 and Manitowoc 25/6 in 2015/2019. That earlier recurrence qualifies lake trout for **research at all four Wisconsin ports**, with weak recent evidence. The tables **pool Algoma with Kewaunee and Two Rivers with Manitowoc**, estimate harvested fish rather than all bites or target effort, and do not give a city pier catch rate. They cannot assign the five salmonids equal strength or support copying offshore lake-trout scores. [DNR harvest tables](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/LM_CreelHarvestTables1998-2024.pdf).

**What the annual timing evidence can and cannot say.** Wisconsin's 2024 report estimates pier harvest of brown trout mainly in March–June, coho in spring and July, Chinook in July–October, and rainbow trout mainly July–September **across all Wisconsin Lake Michigan and Green Bay survey areas**. It has no January–February pier observations and no November–December pier effort in that table. Lake Michigan east-shore clerks worked only May 1–September 30 in 2024; March/April values were modeled, and October pier estimates were omitted. Therefore a tabulated zero, an unsampled month, and a true low-opportunity month are different states. These combined timing rows are candidate shapes only, never five city-specific monthly truths. [DNR 2024 report, methods and Table 6](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/LM_LakeMichiganSportHarvestReport2024.pdf).

**Waukegan's expanded research core.** IDNR describes Waukegan Harbor brown trout and coho in early spring, yellow perch in spring/summer, and returning salmon in fall. A specialist tackle shop's **Government Pier** guide explicitly names coho, Chinook, brown trout, steelhead and perch by season. That exact-pier qualitative list supports research, but is commercial, undated and has no catch denominator. INHS samples Waukegan Harbor **pedestrian** anglers separately from boats, including March; its 2023 report says Waukegan Harbor supplied **55.4% of Illinois pedestrian yellow-perch harvest** during that survey period. Its 2024 abstract aggregates the whole shoreline; do **not** assign statewide coho or boat catches to Government Pier. IDNR's 2024 and 2025 electrofishing finds smallmouth in Waukegan **South Harbor** (13.1 and 11.6 fish/hour of electrofishing) and IDNR generally identifies riprap/piers as smallmouth habitat, enough to research but not to assert an angler rate. [IDNR shore account](https://dnr.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/dnr/oi/documents/april06degrees.pdf); [Government Pier specialist guide](https://lakemichiganangler.com/pages/waukegan_govpier); [INHS 2023 creel report](https://www.ideals.illinois.edu/items/132104/bitstreams/438873/data.pdf); [IDNR lake page](https://ifishillinois.org/Waterbodies/Details/76b7c9b3-4594-4e64-8651-49b0afbdc657); [IDNR 2024 survey](https://www.ifishillinois.org/lmich/reports/SummerHarbor2024.pdf); [IDNR 2025 survey](https://ifishillinois.org/site-documents/SummerHarbor2025.pdf).

**Lake trout, whitefish and smelt require their own checks.** The WI county tables contain **historical pier lake-trout harvest**, so the four Wisconsin lake-trout pairs are research candidates; the 2022–24 zeros prevent a confident modern magnitude. A historical UW Sea Grant overview hosted by NOAA associates Kewaunee with pier/breakwater fishing and lake trout, but does **not** cross-tabulate species by mode. IDNR says lake trout can reach *some* shore structures in winter, without naming Government Pier, so Waukegan remains a lake-trout occurrence lead. Illinois rules and a Waukegan North Harbor advisory name lake/round whitefish, but no Government Pier target series was established; both remain occurrence leads with species identity unresolved. Smelt **is absent from the 19-species PierCast catalog**. IDNR describes a March 1–April 30 net fishery near Waukegan beaches, and the specialist guide lists Government Pier smelt. It is a legitimate separate method-aware catalog research item, but must not be inserted into a rod-and-reel score. The shop's suggestion of May smelt and May 1–June 15 perch conflicts with current state seasons, so those suggested dates are rejected. [UW Sea Grant historical survey](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/40625/noaa_40625_DS1.pdf); [DNR county creel tables](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/LM_CreelHarvestTables1998-2024.pdf); [IDNR lake page](https://ifishillinois.org/Waterbodies/Details/76b7c9b3-4594-4e64-8651-49b0afbdc657); [IDNR smelt guidance](https://ifishillinois.org/Pages/Fishing/WhereToFish/LakeMichigan); [Waukegan advisory](https://www.idph.state.il.us/envhealth/fishadv09/waukegannorthharbor.htm).

## Regulations and report behavior

- Wisconsin and Illinois license/stamp requirements and site-specific seasons must be read from the **2026–27 Wisconsin** and **2026 Illinois** official regulations at scoring/release time. Regulatory permission is a distinct eligibility gate; biological potential must not silently imply legal harvest. [WI regulations portal](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Fishing/regulations); [Illinois regulations](https://dnr.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/dnr/publications/documents/00000953.pdf).
- Illinois yellow perch is closed **May 1–June 15**; the 2026 IDNR waterbody page also states a 15-fish daily limit. No Waukegan report should recommend perch harvest in that window. [IDNR Lake Michigan rules](https://ifishillinois.org/Waterbodies/Details/76b7c9b3-4594-4e64-8651-49b0afbdc657).
- Illinois salmon snagging is restricted to specified sites and dates; Waukegan is limited to the **North Harbor basin** October 1–December 31. It cannot support a Government Pier rod-and-reel score. [IDNR Lake Michigan rules](https://ifishillinois.org/Waterbodies/Details/76b7c9b3-4594-4e64-8651-49b0afbdc657).
- Waukegan smelt is a **March 1–April 30** netting fishery, often at beaches north of town. The Government Pier guide also names smelt, but exact current pier netting practice and intensity need verification. PierCast's current rod-and-reel catalog does not include smelt or its method. Bullheads, carp, rock bass and pumpkinseed mentioned in site sources are separately recorded as **catalog-extension leads**, not mislabeled as channel catfish, bass, or bluegill. [IDNR Lake Michigan guidance](https://ifishillinois.org/Pages/Fishing/WhereToFish/LakeMichigan); [Government Pier guide](https://lakemichiganangler.com/pages/waukegan_govpier); [Wisconsin DNR access guide](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/ClosetoHomeFishing.pdf).

## Pass 1 decision and Pass 2 entry conditions

Pass 1's five city-harbor boundaries, 95-pair disposition, evidence ledger, access concerns, and regulations review are complete. **Algoma remains in the full five-city build.** Construction is report/access metadata; it does not prevent historical, seasonal or current-condition modeling. The report must avoid implying that a closed segment is currently fishable. The precise Manitowoc pier point, Two Rivers pier signage, Kewaunee pier fishing signage, and Waukegan Government Pier/parking rules should be checked for final access text. The remaining held species need the local mode evidence named in the matrix. If evidence stays weak, they remain unscored rather than silently copied from another port.

Pass 2 must inspect site-level Wisconsin creel interview strata where possible, Waukegan pedestrian species rows from INHS creel reports, independent seasonal observations, and current access notices. It should evaluate all **32** candidates and **12** leads, including Waukegan's lake-trout/whitefish questions and the separate smelt method issue, then build supported 365-day curves and compare absolute peak strength against existing Formula v3 cities, including Sheboygan. A large charter catch, statewide monthly estimate, or fish seen by electrofishing is not a substitute for that calibration. No score is approved by this document.
