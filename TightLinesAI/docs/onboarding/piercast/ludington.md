# Ludington PierCast Dossier

**Covered:** North Breakwater only\
**Published access:** yes\
**Live access:** not checked; user must follow current signs and conditions

## Route and identity

Use Stearns Park, 950 W Ludington Ave, Ludington, MI 49431. The city
facility-map pin is `43.9554914532683, -86.4602797427137`. The City identifies
the North Breakwater Light at Stearns Park, and its waterfront plan describes
the beach path leading to the north breakwall for fishing and walking.

The stub-pier alias remains unresolved and the South Breakwater remains
excluded. Neither is counted or displayed as covered.

## Frozen species

Chinook salmon, Coho salmon, Steelhead, and Brown trout are candidate products
with frozen provisional curves. No other species is in v1 scope.

## Temperature validation

LMHOFS cell `(235,159)` remains provisional. GLOS `obs_62` is a supplemental
offshore surface comparison, not a pier thermometer. Local source
`ludington_north_breakwater__surface_logger_v1` must establish lake-side
equivalence.
