# PierCast Scoring v3 — P0 Claim Review

## Decision summary

The nine highest-risk legacy claims should remain available for v3 research, but none should carry its current numeric peak into Formula v3 without recalibration. Eight are admitted secondary-species pairings supported by recurring or corroborated pier catches but lacking standardized local effort. The ninth, Sheboygan Chinook, has unusually strong direct event evidence but lacks the city/month pier effort denominator needed to compare its `9.6` peak cleanly with Michigan's reference fisheries.

This is a claim-preservation decision, not a score-preservation decision. It protects legitimate species coverage while preventing the existence of a legacy curve from becoming evidence for its own magnitude.

## Review standard

P0 claims were reviewed against four separate questions:

1. **Identity:** Is the fish identified to species?
2. **Main-pier relevance:** Does the observation apply to the city's main public pier context?
3. **Recurrence and timing:** Is there more than a one-off occurrence, and is the claimed seasonal mode supported?
4. **Magnitude:** Is comparable effort or another strong denominator available to distinguish an ordinary fishery from a strong or reference-class fishery?

Passing the first three questions retains a pairing for v3 research. It does not automatically pass the fourth. DNR weekly reports are especially useful for species, place, mode, timing, and method, but Michigan DNR describes them as a view of roughly the previous seven days and warns that Great Lakes conditions can change daily or hourly.[^1]

## Cross-pair conclusions

### Admission and magnitude must remain separate

All eight secondary-species pairings have enough evidence to remain research candidates. Their Grade C status reflects magnitude and representation uncertainty, not a conclusion that the fishery is imaginary. Removing them solely because their catch rates are not published would make the product less complete; presenting their current decimals as equally certain would overstate the evidence.

Formula v3 should therefore support an admitted research mode whose promotion eligibility remains false. This is preferable to applying a confidence discount to the number. A hidden discount would make a `4.5` ambiguous: it could mean an ordinary fishery or a strong fishery supported by weak evidence.

### Several legacy maxima require downward as well as upward freedom

The new architecture must not become a one-way scale expansion. Full access to `10` addresses Formula v2's mathematical compression, but individual legacy values must be free to move down when their evidence is occurrence-heavy and effort-light. Manistee perch and Grand Haven drum are the clearest P0 examples because their current peaks enter the strong band without standardized local effort.

Conversely, low legacy ceilings should not be raised merely to make every species visually exciting. Manistee lake trout's limited direct spring record is compatible with a modest maximum. A city can reach Prime through a different, genuinely stronger species.

### Sheboygan Chinook is exceptional but not yet a universal anchor

The September 2026 report supports an exceptional event at the covered piers. It does not by itself establish how frequently that event recurs or whether Sheboygan should permanently outrank Michigan ports with long port-specific Pier/Dock series. The correct v3 treatment is a strong fall-staging mode with its absolute fishery strength calibrated against the common reference set. Temperature can then determine whether a particular day realizes that potential.

## Required closure evidence

The eight secondary-species pairings need the same compact prospective record:

- city, main-pier context, date, and fishing interval;
- identified target species and method;
- observed effort, including unsuccessful trips;
- catches and releases, not harvest alone;
- whether the observation reflects intentional targeting or incidental catch; and
- the same archived LMHOFS issue used by the score.

For P0 calibration, 25–50 well-distributed, effort-backed observations per pairing would be a meaningful magnitude checkpoint, not final predictive validation. Data must include weak days and shoulder periods. Sheboygan Chinook additionally needs multiple seasons or a publishable city-pier extract to establish recurrence frequency at the upper end of the scale.

## P0 disposition

No P0 pairing is deleted. No existing rating is changed. All nine advance to opportunity-mode construction with their existing numbers quarantined as comparators. Final v3 magnitude remains open until the cross-city calibration step.
