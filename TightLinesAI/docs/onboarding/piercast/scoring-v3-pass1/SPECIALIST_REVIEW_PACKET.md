# PierCast Scoring v3 Independent Review Packet

## Review request

FinFindr requests an independent Great Lakes pier-fisheries review of the completed PierCast Scoring v3 Pass 1 package before Formula v3 is eligible for promotion. The reviewer should have professional experience with Lake Michigan salmonid management, creel surveys, shore/pier fisheries, or closely comparable applied fisheries work.

This request does not ask the reviewer to certify catch probabilities or endorse FinFindr’s product. It asks whether the evidence has been transferred responsibly into an ordinal city/species opportunity model.

## Primary questions

1. Are the 36 admitted core-salmonid pairings legitimate recurring targets in the stated general city harbor/main-pier context?
2. Are any of the 29 deferred or 52 excluded pairings clearly misclassified based on authoritative evidence available through September 14, 2026?
3. Does separating fishery strength, seasonal availability, and thermal compatibility avoid material biological misrepresentation?
4. Are the 98 proposed opportunity modes biologically coherent, or should any be merged, split, renamed, or removed?
5. Are the cross-city strength orderings defensible as ordinal product judgments given the different Michigan and Wisconsin survey designs?
6. Should any mode rated `7.0+` be lower because recurring pier-mode evidence is insufficient?
7. Do the four Grade A reference-class modes deserve `9.5–10.0`, especially Manistee steelhead at `10.0`?
8. Is withholding Sheboygan Chinook from the reference-class band at `9.4` appropriately conservative?
9. Are the shared species thermal hypotheses too narrow, too broad, or inappropriate for any mode?
10. Are winter/open-water limitations sufficiently separated from biological availability?

## Required response format

For each concern, provide:

- city and species;
- mode, if applicable;
- finding: `accept`, `revise`, `defer`, or `exclude`;
- evidence or professional basis;
- materiality: `blocking`, `important`, or `minor`;
- recommended correction; and
- whether the issue affects other cities or species.

The reviewer should also state whether any conflict of interest exists and which source files were inspected.

## Promotion rule

A blocking specialist finding prevents Pass 2 promotion of the affected pairing. Corrections must regenerate the decision, calibration, audit, and weekly artifacts. Silence, informal approval, or review of screenshots alone is not sign-off.

No external review has been claimed in the repository. This packet records the exact review requested and prevents an internal calibration from being mislabeled as independent validation.
