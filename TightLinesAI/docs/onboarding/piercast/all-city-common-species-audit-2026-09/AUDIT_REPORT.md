# PierCast all-city common-species calibration audit

Completed September 17, 2026.

Scope: all 17 established and current private-onboarding cities, audited for Chinook salmon, coho salmon, steelhead, brown trout, lake trout and freshwater drum.

Future work must follow the repository-wide [calibration and research standard](../CALIBRATION_AND_RESEARCH_STANDARD.md), which preserves the exact research, cross-city anchoring, missing-evidence and 10.0-cap rules established by this audit.

Visibility: the corrected calibration is active for the established 12-city public Formula v3 catalog. Two Rivers, Kewaunee, Algoma, Manitowoc and Waukegan remain private onboarding work and are absent from the public catalog. The append-only v6 evidence run remains blocked from direct promotion.

## Result

The concern was valid. Eighteen established-Wisconsin salmonid peaks were unfairly low because the earlier Wisconsin expansion used missing city-level effort and magnitude precision as a reason to lower `F`. The frozen Formula v3 contract requires evidence confidence to remain separate from fishery strength.

The error did not affect every city:

- The original Ludington, Grand Haven, Manistee and Frankfort–Elberta core salmonid peaks were retained. They are anchored to local Michigan DNR Pier/Dock catch estimates, matched all-species effort and multi-year recurrence.
- The later lake-trout and freshwater-drum peaks were retained. Their lower values follow low local pier catch rates, sparse positive strata, or explicit qualitative magnitude such as `some`; they are not confidence discounts.
- The Lake Huron values were retained. Repeated exact-port reports support the admitted rows, while direct descriptions such as `few`, `occasional`, `some`, `slow` and `good` constrain their magnitude.
- All five current Pass 2 city calibrations were retained after comparison with the corrected established-city order.

The completed grid contains 102 city/species cells: 18 revised numeric rows, 59 retained numeric rows and 25 evidence holds with no numeric score. A hold is not a low rating. It means recurring intentional local main-pier opportunity is not established well enough to admit the pair.

The Lake Michigan Michigan-port order is supported by low modern Pier/Dock catch rates of roughly 1.6 to 4.1 per 1,000 all-species hours and only three to five positive matched strata. Lake Huron's higher ordinary-band values reflect explicit recurring port opportunity and exact nearshore reports. No Wisconsin city inherited offshore lake-trout strength: Wisconsin DNR estimated only three lake trout harvested from piers statewide in 2024.

### Freshwater drum

Grand Haven 7.2; Ludington 5.0; Manistee 4.7; Oscoda 3.9.

Grand Haven's 7.2 is supported by 18,252 estimated Pier/Dock drum across 246,982 matched hours and 43 of 57 positive strata. Ludington and Manistee have much lower rates and recurrence. Oscoda has repeated exact-pier reports but qualitative low magnitude. Other cities remain unscored until repeated intentional local pier targeting establishes admission and seasonal shape.

## Guardrail now enforced

1. Determine whether the city/species pier pairing is admitted.
2. For an admitted pair, set `F` from the best recurring local opportunity and explicit stronger/weaker same-species anchors.
3. Put window width and recurrence timing in `A`.
4. Record missing effort or magnitude precision in evidence grade and limitations. Do not reduce `F` because the row is Grade B.
5. Use explicit event magnitude such as `few`, `occasional`, `some`, `slow`, measured low catch rate or repeated zero estimates as fishery evidence where applicable. These are observed constraints rather than generic uncertainty.
6. Leave an unsupported pair without a numeric score. Do not create a low number from regional, boat or offshore occurrence.
