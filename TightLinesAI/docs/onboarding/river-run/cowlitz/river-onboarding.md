## 2. Identity and corridor

The product begins at the official mouth boundary and ends below the Barrier Dam exclusion, about 51 miles. It does not pretend adults swim through Mayfield or Mossyrock. Fish transported to the Tilton, Cispus, upper Cowlitz, or Lake Scanewa are separate managed dispositions.

Public copy must direct users to current WDFW reach rules/emergency changes, identify the Barrier exclusion without turning it into access guidance, and state that hydropower releases can change conditions quickly. ### Fish Counts capability

The Cowlitz Salmon Hatchery adult separator is eligible as a `separator_recovery` source. Official reports supply weekly and season totals, hatchery/natural origin, five-year comparisons, and dispositions. “Total Returns” means adults collected at the separator. Recycling/transport destination columns can include recaptures and must never be summed into Total Returns. Favor weekly new recoveries plus season total, observed-through date, operating days, origin split, and source link. The application requests the official report on every report load with cache bypass, so an updated weekly report can appear immediately; Tacoma Power—not the app—sets the reporting cadence. State that fish below the separator, harvested below it, or not recovered are absent. Counts never affect Stage, Activity, Presence, or catch probability.
