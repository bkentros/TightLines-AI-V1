# Bois Brule Four-species Timing Audit

**Researched:** 2026-08-26 **Status:** `gate_4a_owner_review`

## Accepted calendar findings

| Species              | Agency season / peak evidence                                                                                | Runtime anchor | Reconciliation                                                                                                       |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chinook              | early July-mid-October; peak mid-August-late September                                                       | Sept. 5        | conservative midpoint inside the direct agency peak; small sectional run                                             |
| Coho                 | late August-late November; agency summary says September                                                     | Sept. 28       | 2023 week 39 and 2024 last-week-September charts agree; 2025 late-Sept.-mid-Oct. strength is preserved as a shoulder |
| Fall Steelhead       | mid-August-late November; extensive movement mid-Sept.-late Oct.; broader maximum late Sept.-first half Nov. | **Sept. 28**   | owner/local exact fall-entry anchor, consistent with agency window; not a spring-spawning or departure date          |
| Lake-run Brown Trout | early July-late October; peak mid-July-mid-September                                                         | Aug. 15        | independent Lake Superior calendar, substantially earlier than Lake Michigan runs                                    |

Fishway passage timing is biased by structure location and passage behavior. It
does not prove first mouth arrival, whole-river abundance, catch rate, or equal
section distribution. The calendar combines the agency's explicit run windows
with current annual passage charts and keeps those meanings distinct.
