### Required fixed replay and controlled tests

Activity remains blocked until a production-shaped replay is generated and
owner-reviewed. The Open-Meteo archive probe returned all required time,
precipitation, cloud-cover, and shortwave arrays for September 15–16, 2007, but
returned the requested clear-sky-radiation array entirely `null`. The existing
engine can fall back to cloud cover plus shortwave, but replay must treat this
as an explicit source mode and verify it rather than claiming normalized
actual-versus-clear-sky light. Lock 2007–2025 before examining distributions;
if the full audit finds a material schema/method discontinuity, document and
version the longest reliable >=5-year interval rather than cherry-picking.

The replay artifact must report:

- Expected dates and usable dates/coverage; missing hourly weather by date and
  block. Flow, prior-flow, temperature, and lookback counts must explicitly
  report `not inputs`, not zero missing measurements.
- Daily/block min, p10, median, p90, max, unique scores, and label distribution
  overall and for beginning/building/peak/tapering/ending.
- Best-block frequencies; leader ties; block-spread median/p90/max.
- Clear/cloud/light and dry/trace/moderate/heavy in-block precipitation
  subsets; there are no warm/cold/flow subsets because those inputs are absent.
- Counts at the 90/85 caps and proof that tomorrow never exceeds its cap.
- Identical-weather lifecycle trace from 10-28 through 12-01 with no
  unexplained one-day cliff.
- Single-block cloud and rain isolation, daily-rollup bounds, missing-weather
  behavior, today/tomorrow rollover, DST, and completed-block freezing.
- Zero failures for weather-only disclosure, Limited confidence, section/weir
  scope, foreign geography/species, abundance/movement/catch/safety language,
  internal leakage, and post-completion scores.

Calibration changes after replay must be versioned with biological/product
rationale. Do not tune merely to produce attractive distributions.
