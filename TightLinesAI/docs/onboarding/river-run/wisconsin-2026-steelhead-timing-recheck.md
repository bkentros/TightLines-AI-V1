# Wisconsin Four-river Steelhead Timing Recheck

> **Superseded for peak timing on 2026-08-27.** The owner supplied exact
> river-specific fall Steelhead peak dates from a local Wisconsin angler. See
> `wisconsin-2026-steelhead-local-peak-calibration.md`. This document remains as
> the research audit trail and no longer controls the implemented calendars.

**Completed:** 2026-08-26
**Question:** Are the hidden fall Steelhead calendars materially too early?
**Decision:** Retain all four calendars; clarify that the early Lake Michigan
dates model a low Skamania leading edge, not the main fall fishery.

## Primary-source findings

1. Wisconsin DNR's current 2026 fishery report says Skamania run from late
   summer through fall, Chambers Creek from late fall through spring, and
   Ganaraska in spring. It also says the fishery is currently dominated by the
   latter two strains but that Skamania were stocked again in 2022.
2. DNR's Steelhead strain guide describes Skamania migration beginning as early
   as late June/early July while emphasizing that good stream fishing normally
   begins only after cooling in mid-September. Chambers Creek's strongest
   stream period is mid-November through December; Ganaraska adds November-
   December pulses before its spring phase.
3. DNR's 2024-2025 Root River facility report identifies all 41 coded-wire-tag
   Steelhead recovered in fall 2024 as age-three Skamania from the 2021 year
   class stocked in 2022. With the untagged/clip categories included, 49 of 60
   processed fall Steelhead were adipose-only. The report says the Wisconsin
   members of that returning cohort were stocked in the Sheboygan, Milwaukee,
   and Root rivers.
4. DNR directly states that Bois Brule fall Steelhead begin moving in mid-
   August, continue through late November, and are most extensive from mid-
   September through late October.

## Calendar decisions

| River | Hidden calendar interpretation | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Milwaukee | Aug. 1 staging, Aug. 15 low beginning, meaningful build after Sept. 1, strongest Skamania-weighted window from mid-Sept. into Oct., then a long multi-strain shoulder | retain |
| Sheboygan | Aug. 1 staging and Aug. 15 low beginning represent the stocked Skamania edge; the November anchor represents Chambers/Ganaraska contribution rather than a claim that summer is the main run | retain |
| Root | July staging and Aug. 1 beginning are deliberately low-presence Skamania phases; fall 2024 cohort evidence directly confirms the early-running strain, while the November-December shoulder preserves the winter-run strains | retain |
| Bois Brule | Mid-August start and mid-Sept.-late-Oct. high window directly match the current river-specific DNR calendar | retain |

The app must not translate `staging` or `beginning` into “the main run is on.”
For the three Lake Michigan rivers, user-facing guidance should treat mid-
September as the first generally credible fall-fishing threshold unless current
cooling and direct river evidence support an earlier opportunity.
