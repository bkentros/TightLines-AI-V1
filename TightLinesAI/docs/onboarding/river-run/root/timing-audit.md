# Root River Four-species Timing Audit

**Completed:** 2026-08-26 **Gate:** `4A hidden candidate`

## Bias controls

The Root River Steelhead Facility is an operational sampler, not a continuous
mouth counter. In fall 2025 it operated Sept. 16-Nov. 10; in 2024 it operated
Sept. 23-Nov. 11; and in 2023 it operated Sept. 25-Nov. 13. DNR states that fish
can pass before the facility operates and can bypass it during high flow.
Therefore installation, processing, spawning, egg-take, and shutdown dates
bound observations but do not define first river entry, peak passage, or the
last fish in the river.

The strongest direct calendar statement is DNR's fall 2025 finding that large
numbers moved upstream in early-to-mid October despite low water and an initially
warm river. That statement anchors the salmon high period. Coho remains later
than Chinook because the facility's Coho egg-take program and regional Coho
biology extend the dependable pulse later into October.

## Accepted Gate 4A calendars

| Species | Start | Broad build | Peak band / anchor | Main end | Tail meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chinook | Aug. 25 | Sept. 20 | Oct. 1-18 / Oct. 8 | Nov. 10 | terminal residual estimate through Nov. 20 |
| Coho | Sept. 5 | Sept. 25 | Oct. 5-25 / Oct. 15 | Nov. 20 | terminal residual estimate through Nov. 30 |
| Fall Steelhead | Sept. 1 | Sept. 25 | Oct. 10-Oct. 31 / **Oct. 10** | Dec. 31 | owner/local exact peak; living fish may overwinter or enter later phases |
| Lake-run Brown Trout | Oct. 1 | Nov. 1 | Nov. 15-Dec. 15 / Nov. 30 | Jan. 15 | model ends; survivors may hold or return lakeward |

Chinook and Coho use semelparous terminal decline. Steelhead combines early
Skamania context with stronger late-fall Chambers Creek/Ganaraska entry but is
not a spring model. Brown timing is intentionally later than the salmon model:
DNR's 2023-2025 reports state that the facility normally closes before most
Brown Trout are in the Root, while current brood work supports a
November-to-mid-December high period. Brown Trout never receive salmon mortality
copy.

## Primary evidence

- Wisconsin DNR, [Fall 2025 Root River Weir Summary](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/LM_RootRiverSummaryFall2025.pdf): operation dates, early/mid-October movement, and current species totals.
- Wisconsin DNR, [Fall 2024 Root River Weir Summary](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/LM_RootRiverSummaryFall2024.pdf): operation dates, October movement, and low-water limitation.
- Wisconsin DNR, [Fall 2024/Spring 2025 facility report](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/LM_RootRiverSteelheadFacilityFall2024Spring2025.pdf): ten-year series, subset/bypass warning, Brown shutdown bias, and Steelhead strain composition.
- Wisconsin DNR, [Fall 2023 Root River Weir Summary](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/LM_RootRiverSummaryFall2023.pdf): independent operation window and totals.
- Wisconsin DNR, [Steelhead strain guide](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/sites/default/files/topic/Fishing/Species_steelhead.pdf): strain-specific entry/spawn timing and repeat-spawner lifecycle.
- Wisconsin DNR, [Lake Michigan trout and salmon questions](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Fishing/questions/lakemichtroutsalmon.html): post-spawn Brown Trout survival and possible lakeward return.

These dates remain calibrated seasonal boundaries rather than a promise that
fish enter or leave on a specific day.
