## 1. Normative public geography

Use these exact section names in every River Run primitive:

1. **Lower river (M-55–Bear Creek)**
2. **Middle river (Bear Creek–High Bridge)**
3. **Upper river (High Bridge–Tippy Dam)**
4. **Manistee Lake, harbor, and river entrance** — staging context only; it is
   not proof of river entry.

The Upper river includes the entire High Bridge–Tippy Dam corridor. Public copy
should emphasize the Tippy Dam area when accumulated or peak fish are the most
useful starting read, but must not present `Tippy tailwater` as a fourth river
section. `Upper river` never means water above Tippy Dam.

Stage copy names one primary section. It may name one secondary section only
with a clear trigger. Habitat and tactics belong in Guide's Read, not in Where
to Start.

## 2. Barrier and endpoint

Tippy Dam is the upstream terminus of the fall migratory corridor and blocks
upstream passage by Chinook, Coho, and Steelhead. River Run must never recommend
water above it or imply that fish pass it.

- Barrier ID: `big_manistee_tippy_dam`
- Passage status: impassable
- Public upstream limit: yes
- Copy phrase: `below Tippy Dam` or `Upper river (High Bridge–Tippy Dam)`
- Safety: do not invent a fixed distance closure; follow current signs, booms,
  DNR notices, and dam-safety information.

Supporting DNR sources:

- [Slagle Creek Status Report 2014-183](https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/dnr/Documents/Fisheries/Status/folder2/2014-183.pdf?rev=95e8763b6b3d462aae26772e53b4206a)
- [Manistee River, Hodenpyl to Red Bridge Status Report 2004-2](https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/dnr/Documents/Fisheries/Status/folder4/StatusReport_ManisteeRiverHodenpylRedBridge_04-2.pdf?rev=104a39031ffc4be3a63b1d8b0efe60d8)

## 3. Source coverage

USGS 04125550 is approximately 700 feet below Tippy Dam. It provides the
accepted discharge and measured-water-temperature inputs. It represents the
Upper river, especially the Tippy Dam area; it does not directly measure the
Middle river, Lower river, Manistee Lake, harbor, or river entrance.

Consequences:

| Primitive | Public scope |
| --- | --- |
| Migration Stage | Whole accepted corridor; seasonal geography, not live confirmation |
| Migration Timing | Season-to-date Wellston/Upper-river evidence |
| Push | Current fresh-movement support in the Upper river |
| Fishability | Current presentation shape in the Upper river |
| Activity | Responsiveness conditions in the Upper river |
| Fish In River | Whole-corridor seasonal presence estimate, not equal distribution |

The Sherman gauge is context only and must never be blended into or presented
as the live migratory-corridor read.

## 4. Species truths and terminal behavior

Accepted calendars, thresholds, presence ceilings, and primitive
determinations remain unchanged by this copy pass unless a separate scoring
defect is proven.

### Chinook

- Strong, broadly distributed fall opportunity; 100/100 river ceiling.
- Early river entry begins August 15; peak reference September 30.
- Main run ends October 31; sparse tail ends November 10.
- Terminal state: `Fall run complete`, no score, and return when staging begins
  in early August.

### Coho

- Moderate, sectional fall opportunity; 50/100 river ceiling.
- Sparse river entry begins September 10; peak reference October 20.
- Main run ends November 30; seasonal presence reaches zero December 10.
- Terminal state: `Fall run complete`, no score, and return when staging begins
  in early September.

### Steelhead

- Strong, broad fall-entry opportunity; 80/100 river ceiling.
- Early Steelhead may enter in September. Describe them only as `early
  Steelhead`; do not infer a separate or established summer run from their date
  or location.
- The accepted fall-entry curve begins September 15, peaks November 15, and
  ends after December 22.
- Terminal state: `Fall entry complete`, no score, no winter recommendation,
  and return when staging tracking resumes in early September.
- Historical strain information may remain in internal research notes, but
  public copy must not use `Skamania`, `summer-run`, or `winter-run` to classify
  a fish in this Big Manistee fall experience.

## 5. State-copy contract

### Migration Stage progression

- Before entry: Manistee Lake, harbor, and river entrance.
- Beginning: Lower river primary; Middle river may be the one conditional add.
- Early building: Middle river primary; Upper river may be conditional.
- Broad building and peak: Upper river primary with Tippy Dam area emphasis;
  Middle river may be the comparison for fresher or less accumulated fish.
- Tapering and ending: Upper river primary, especially the Tippy Dam area;
  add Middle river only for established holding water or direct activity.
- Complete: no active starting section.

Coho copy must preserve limited/sectional qualification. Stage does not claim
equal fish numbers in every section.

### Migration Timing

Ahead, Typical, Delayed, mixed, reversal-tempered, evaluating, insufficient,
and complete states must say they are based on season-to-date Wellston flow and
measured temperature. The geographic adjustment is capped within the three
approved sections. Timing does not locate fish or prove current movement.

### Push

Why copy uses at most three short points: current Wellston hydraulic response,
measured temperature, and the material rain/cap/freshness qualification. It
supports possible fresh movement in the Upper river; it does not prove new
arrivals anywhere in the corridor.

### Fishability

Why copy normally uses three short points: flow band, direction/freshness, and
Upper-river scope. It determines presentation shape, not abundance, activity,
access, or safety.

### Activity

Why copy uses no more than three points: overall responsiveness, strongest
time block or tied leaders, and lifecycle/confidence/scope. A time block is
called strongest only when it leads the runner-up by at least three points.
All river measurements are Upper-river/Tippy-area measurements.

### Fish In River

The internal score remains exact. The public score is state-preserving and
rounded to the nearest five. Copy explains seasonal presence relative to this
river's species-specific ceiling; it is not a count, live report, or claim of
equal distribution.

## 6. Copy QA prohibitions

Big Manistee public copy must reject:

- foreign river names or landmarks;
- `Tippy tailwater` as a fourth public section;
- vague `lower corridor`, `middle corridor`, `lakeward`, or `toward M-55`
  location plans without the approved section name;
- any recommendation above Tippy Dam or claim that fish pass it;
- Wellston measurements applied to the Middle or Lower river;
- public `Skamania`, `summer-run`, `winter-run`, winter-holding, or promised
  winter-experience copy;
- more than three Why points, filler, or repeated scope disclaimers;
- a strongest Activity window when the lead is under three points;
- a score in a completed salmon run or completed Steelhead fall-entry state.

## 7. Current regulations

The accepted legal reach is Tippy Dam downstream to the railroad bridge below
M-55. It is Type 3 water with a year-round one-Rainbow-Trout possession limit
and special artificial-lure/terminal-gear rules from August 1 through November
15. Regulations, posted boundaries, closures, and access must be checked at
release time.
