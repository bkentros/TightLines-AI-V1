# Color Match: bait-specific familiarity review

September 8, 2026. Approved and implemented. Original review and pre-change inventory are retained below for traceability. See implementation record at the end.

Scope: all 23 live bait choices, union of their six clarity/light pools. The inventory below is exported from the current compiled matrix, not the unused research candidates. A manufacturer's color chart establishes existence, not ownership frequency or suitability in every condition. The shortlist is editorial judgment for the user's more conservative selection, not a measured popularity ranking. Failure to find an exact example is not proof that nobody makes it.

## Evidence and limits

- [Yamamoto 5-inch Senko](https://www.yamamotobaits.com/products/5-senko/): 020 Black, 002 Smoke, 523 black-blue body/clear-blue tail, 231 plum/emerald, 900 Red Shad, 918 PB&J. The existing broad worm choice includes stick, finesse, ribbon-tail and Ned shapes, but uses the stick-worm pool; a color's availability on one shape does not prove it for all shapes.
- [Zoom color register](https://zoombait.com/colors/): distinguishes Brown (special-distributor color), Cinnamon Black, Chartreuse Pearl, Black and other recipes. This register alone is not bait-specific evidence.
- [Zoom Super Fluke at Tackle Warehouse](https://www.tacklewarehouse.com/Zoom_Super_Fluke/descpage-ZSF.html): Chartreuse Pearl, not automatic proof of our flat chartreuse recipe.
- [Keitech Swing Impact Black](https://www.keitechusa.com/keitech-swimbaits/keitech-swing-impact-4-black.html): exact solid-black paddletail.
- [Keitech Swing Impact range](https://www.keitechusa.com/catalog/swing-impact.html): distinguishes black, black-blue flake, chartreuse shad, chartreuse-white and electric-blue/chartreuse.
- [Mister Twister 2026 Curly Tail catalog](https://www.mistertwister.com/catalog/2026/MisterTwister2026Catalog_16.pdf): black, Junebug, smoke flake, pumpkin pepper and other separately named colors.
- [Megabass Vision Oneten](https://megabassusa.com/product/vision-oneten/): bait-specific reference; a color called Deadly Black Shad must not be treated as evidence of an all-black body.
- [Rapala Firetiger Shadow Rap](https://blog.rapala.com/news/new-color-patterns-for-rapala-shad-dancer-shadow-rap-and-super-shad-rap/): exact jerkbait family and Firetiger recipe.
- [SPRO KGB Chad Shad 180](https://www.spro.com/collections/hard-baits/products/kgb-chad-shad-180): Fire Tiger and Sun Chart are actually listed alongside forage colors. Bright glides are not automatically implausible or exclusive to pike.
- [Umpqua Shenandoah Popper](https://www.umpqua.com/shenandoah-popper//): Blue, freshwater bass.
- [Orvis smallmouth popper guidance](https://news.orvis.com/fly-fishing/pro-tips-low-water-tactics-for-smallmouth-bass/): yellow, white, black and blue.
- [Orvis summer bass flies](https://howtoflyfish.orvis.com/video-lessons/1451-14--flies-for-summer-bass): yellow/orange/green/black; general color guidance, not exact two-tone construction proof.
- [Umpqua 2024 catalog, externally hosted](https://projecthealingwaters.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Umpqua-Catalog-2024.pdf): Black/Purple Agent Scully streamer.
- [Mepps 2026 Black Fury catalog](https://www.mepps.com/catalog/2026/MeppsCatalog2026_11.pdf): dark decorated blade family. Manufacturer product page was also indexed but direct access returned 403.
- [Strike King Red Eye Shad at Discount Tackle](https://discounttackle.com/products/strike-king-red-eyed-shad-lipless-crankbait-2-25-inch): Clear Ghost Sexy Shad exists on lipless baits; our label Ghost minnow could be simplified to Ghost shad for this type, without pretending transparency is an invented lipless finish.

## Next step after user decisions

Apply exclusions per bait, not globally. Recheck each clarity/light pool for at least two distinct eligible colors, preserving uniform random selection and the daily cache. If a pool becomes too small, surface it rather than adding filler. Do not silently replace Black with Junebug or a black-back pattern; those are different recipes.

## Approved implementation

Applied all A1–A12 removals per bait, plus the separately flagged solid-black craw/creature and tube choices. Kept solid-black worms, paddletails and grubs; Junebug grubs; blue fly poppers; Firetiger jerkbaits and hard swimbaits; black/purple streamers; decorated black spinner blades. Retained canonical recipes for other bait types and saved reports.

The user separately approved Gold/brass alongside Firetiger for murky-water spoons after the removals left only Firetiger. This explicit allowlist applies to the spoon category and its legacy spoon types. It is not automatic pool padding. Gold is an existing spoon finish; extending its close-range flash rationale to murky water is editorial guidance, not experimentally demonstrated superiority. [Mepps spoon guidance](https://www.mepps.com/products/brochures/walleye.pdf) describes gold under overcast skies; [Acme](https://www.acmetackle.com/blogs/news/acme-spoons-for-ice-fishing) describes gold finishes reflecting available light. Neither establishes a guaranteed murky-water visibility range.

Catalog version: 2026-09-08.1. Selection version and daily lock remain unchanged. New reports use curated pools; immutable saved reports retain original selections. No database cache reset or reroll was performed.

Current matrix: 23 choices, 138 live cells, 560 eligible color occurrences (previously 652). Twelve live cells have exactly two colors. Their pair cannot vary, but the uniform shuffle can change the order. Every pool supports two distinct picks; the outdated three-color minimum was replaced by the current two-choice requirement. Exclusions also apply to mapped legacy types so older entry paths cannot bypass curation.

Validation: 34 tests pass, including explicit removed/retained pattern checks, two-choice order variation, all 336 legacy cells, all 138 live cells, visibility copy, daily locking and replay. Structural/domain QA passes. Regenerated `live_picker_pools.json` is the current inventory.

Deployment complete: color-picker function updated on the existing Supabase project. TypeScript and whitespace checks passed. Live Open-Meteo generation confirmed the new catalog version, no plain black/brown craw choices, the exact Gold/brass + Firetiger murky spoon pair, immutable daily replay, concurrent winner and separate-bait reports. Temporary test account and all three reports were deleted afterward.
