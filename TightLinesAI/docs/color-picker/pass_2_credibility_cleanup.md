## Family review

| Family | Disposition and rationale |
| --- | --- |
| Soft plastics | Retained known earth tones, dark colors, flakes and laminates, including Junebug, PB&J and red shad. Exact variants are described; palette transfer between compatible worm shapes remains explicit. No invented brand name or color-changing pigment. |
| Skirted jigs and hair jigs | Retained familiar brown, green pumpkin, black/blue, white/chartreuse, PB&J and forage palettes where explicitly bound. Natural hair/fiber and silicone/plastic remain separate. |
| Spinnerbaits, vibrating jigs and buzzbaits | Recommend recognizable skirt colors. Remove hardware-only variations; optional coordinated trailers are illustrative examples rather than a uniquely required product. |
| Underspins and tail spinners | Retained pale, olive, golden-shiner, chartreuse and perch palettes on compatible bodies. Removed unsupported white-blade variants; head/body color stays distinct from incidental hardware. |
| Inline and bucktail spinners | Retained familiar metal finishes and dark dotted/Firetiger blades. Here the blade is a principal colored lure surface, so its finish remains meaningful. Dressed versions use hair, not invented plastic scales. |
| Hard baits | Retained known metallic, ghost, bone, black, perch, bluegill, craw and Firetiger recipes within explicit shape bindings. No duplicate bone/pearl padding of surface pools. Firetiger retains green/yellow/black/orange placement. |
| Metal lures | Retained familiar silver, brass/gold, copper and painted finishes. A plain metal finish remains a legitimate color choice; no transparent spoon. |
| Frogs and soft toads | Narrowed to black, white and green/yellow underside palettes. No added stripe to force rotation; material and silhouette distinguish a hollow frog from a soft toad. |
| Streamers and crawfish flies | Retained documented fiber palettes and construction-compatible contrast. Craws have claws/legs; sculpins have broad heads. Purple/copper flash is retained because a Kreelex catalog palette supports it, not because of an invented name. |
| Fly poppers | Retained familiar solid and two-tone popper palettes, including documented blue poppers. An unfamiliar color to one angler is not by itself grounds for removal; source support and fly construction matter. |

## Evidence and limits

References live with pattern/profile records and in the generated source register. Added direct support for [white/chartreuse underspin heads](https://mustad-fishing.com/us/products/usj786), [white/chartreuse underbody-spinner combinations](https://midwestoutdoors.com/fishing/spring-spinner-fishing-crappies/), [green-pumpkin bladed jigs](https://zmanfishing.com/blogs/news/early-spring-chatterbait-tactics), and [Bluegill spinnerbaits](https://www.basspro.com/shop/en/z-man-slingbladez-tandem-spinnerbait-3-8-oz-bluegill-2587646).

The catalog defines realistic representative recipes, not exact brand replicas. Familiar colors can still be debated for a particular clarity/light combination; these are sourced heuristics, not proven equal catch rates. Artwork is specified but awaits reference inspection and generated-image acceptance in the image pass.

## Checks

QA rejects the retired IDs, duplicate visible color names within a pool, and incidental blades leaking into body/skirt color components. Image manifests carry separate hardware instructions. Every released type still has all six condition cells with at least three distinct choices. Small pools remain honestly small; refresh must not promise a new combination when all three are already shown.
